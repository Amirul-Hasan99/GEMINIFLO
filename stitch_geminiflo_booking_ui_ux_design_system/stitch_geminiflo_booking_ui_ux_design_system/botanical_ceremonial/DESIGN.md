## Brand & Style

The design system conveys celebratory reverence, artisanal prestige, and effortless hospitality. It serves individuals, event planners, and corporate liaisons ordering artisanal ceremonial flower boards (*papan bunga*) for pivotal life moments: weddings, grand openings, commemorations, and condolences.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Editorial Luxury** with **Warm Organic Minimalism**:
- Pristine, warm backdrops replace stark sterile whites, lending an organic tactile paper quality.
- High-contrast visual structure balances deep botanical forest tones with luminous, champagne-tinted metallics.
- Typography captures ritual importance through elegant high-contrast serif titling juxtaposed with modern geometric sans-serif execution for streamlined commerce workflows.
- Visual motifs incorporate fine linear filigree, gentle botanical curves, and structured editorial framing rather than kitsch floral patterns.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional cadence rooted in ceremonial depth and natural opulence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F382A` / `#154D39`)**: Deep Emerald. Used for high-emphasis containers, primary actions, and hero surfaces to evoke longevity, premium craftsmanship, and authority.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / `#D4AF37`)**: Muted Champagne Gold. Reserved for accents, VIP tier badges, active indicators, luxury borders, and delicate celebratory motifs.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FBF9F5` base, `#F5EFE6` elevated)**: Soft Cream and Warm Alabaster. Provides a warm, tactile foundation reminiscent of heavyweight cotton stationery.
- **Text & Contrast (`#1F2421` primary, `#565E59` secondary)**: Charcoal Forest. Delivers rigorous contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E8E2D8`)**: Linen Gray-Beige. Provides structural clarity while remaining soft and understated.

### Semantic Status Tokens
- **Available / Confirmed**: `#1F6F43` (Forest Sage) with soft background `#E9F5EE`.
- **Limited / Awaiting Verification**: `#9E6E18` (Warm Amber) with soft background `#FEF6E7`.
- **Unavailable / Cancelled**: `#9C2A2A` (Muted Crimson) with soft background `#FDF0F0`.

## Typography

The type system creates an editorial rhythm between celebratory ceremony and transactional efficiency.

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display)**: Infuses ceremonial gravitas into category labels (*Pernikahan, Grand Opening, Duka Cita*), product titles, and editorial announcements. Use italic styling sparingly for accent words or secondary sub-headers.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, modern, and open. Maintains clarity across dimension selectors, slot scheduling grids, shipping coordinates, and text inscription previews.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing**: Micro-labels, status tags, and currency markers use uppercase formatting with wide tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to complement the refined aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first philosophy prioritizing vertical storytelling, high-resolution visual boards, and friction-free configuration workflows.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (<640px)**: 4-column fluid grid. Section margin is fixed at `1.25rem` (20px) with `1rem` gutters. Interactive cards stretch full width or align horizontally inside swipeable editorial carousels.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout. Section margin scales to `2rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Product listings transition cleanly to a balanced 2-column or 3-column arrangement.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm follows an 8px base rhythm.
- Dense layout regions (e.g., character count counters, ribbon text configuration, delivery timeslots) utilize `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Card interiors apply `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) for breathing room around floral photography.
- Distinct functional modules on screens maintain `space-xl` (40px) separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile layering over deep dropshadows, ensuring an organic paper-like and editorial finish.

- **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F5`)**: Ground tier holding layout structures.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Product cards, configuration sheets, and selection chips. Framed by a delicate `1px` border (`#E8E2D8`) and ambient warm drop-shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#F5EFE6`)**: Inset forms, date-time calendar pickers, and summary panels. Features a soft interior contrast with no external drop shadow.
- **Floating Overlays & Sheets**: Pinned booking bars, modals, and sticky checkout summaries sit at top z-index with an elevated shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 56, 42, 0.12)`, topped with a `1px` border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The visual geometry employs an exact 16px radius (`rounded-lg`) on major structural cards, balancing contemporary software precision with organic botanical curves.

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: `16px` (`rounded-lg`). Creates a cohesive silhouette for flower board previews, package selections, and invoice summaries.
- **Action Buttons & Form Fields**: `12px` to `16px`. Provides a generous, tactile touch target.
- **Status Tags & Category Filter Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical taxonomies from structural cards.
- **Board Aspect Ratios**: Papan bunga media cards strictly honor standardized proportions (4:3 horizontal banners or 1:1 detail zooms), enclosed in smooth corner containers with no clipping artifacts.
